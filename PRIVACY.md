# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026. Applies to AttendEase 1.0.0 and later._

AttendEase is an offline Android app. It is an independent student project, not a service:
there is no server, no account and no company behind it.

## What the app collects

**Nothing is collected by anyone.** The app has **no internet permission**. Android itself
stops it from sending data anywhere. It contains no ads, analytics, crash-reporting service or
tracking code.

## What is stored on your phone

When you upload an attendance report PDF, the app saves, in its private storage on your phone:

- **From the report:** the details at the top of the PDF (name, roll number, programme, semester and report dates) and each lecture row (subject, date, time, status).
- **What you add:** lectures, subject names, weekly patterns, medical-leave requests and notes you type.
- **Your settings:** attendance target, semester length, theme, portal address.
- **Crash report:** if the app crashes, a short technical report (app version, Android version, device model and the error). It contains no attendance data. You are offered to copy it on the next start, and it is deleted after that.

The PDF itself is not kept. It is read once and a temporary copy is deleted straight away.

Other apps cannot read this storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, as does
**Settings → Clear all data**.

## When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose:

- **CSV export:** you pick where to send it through Android's share sheet.
- **SAP portal / links:** these open in your web browser. The browser, not AttendEase, connects to those sites, under the browser's own privacy policy.
- **Android backup:** if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android includes the app's data in your phone's backup to **your own Google account**, as it does for other apps. This lets your attendance come back on a new phone. Guest-mode data, the guest-mode switch and crash reports are excluded from backup and phone-to-phone transfer.

## Guest mode

Guest mode keeps a friend's upload in a separate database. Your own data is never opened.
The friend's data is erased when guest mode ends, or automatically if it has not been used for six hours.

## Children

The app is meant for college students. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

Any change to this policy is published in the source repository with the release that
introduces it.
